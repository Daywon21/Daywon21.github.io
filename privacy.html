Privacy policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026. Apps: Vega (SquawkBot) and Captain Multi Chat.

These are desktop programs that run on your computer. They connect streaming accounts so live chat can appear in the app and in OBS. This policy explains what Google account data they access.

Who controls the data

You run the apps on your PC. Google and YouTube tokens stay on that computer. We do not run a cloud account that receives your live chat.

Google account data we access

When you connect YouTube, the app may access:

  • Your Google account email, name, and profile, to show which account is connected
  • Your YouTube channel id and channel name
  • Your live broadcasts and live chat, to show chat while you are live
  • Permission to send live chat messages you or the bot post

The sign-in requests openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile, youtube.readonly, and youtube.force-ssl. The apps do not read your private videos, playlists, or Google Drive. We do not sell Google or YouTube data. We do not use it for advertising.

How it is used

  • Show YouTube live chat while you are streaming
  • Send chat you type, or that Vega posts, to your YouTube live chat
  • Keep you signed in on this PC until you log out

Storage

Tokens stay on your PC until you log out in the app or delete the app data folder. Vega stores them in the .squawkbot folder in your user profile. Captain Multi Chat stores them in an encrypted vault in your user profile.

Your choices

  • Log out of YouTube inside the app
  • Remove the app from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Children

These apps are for streamers and are not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email listed for this app on the Google sign-in screen.
